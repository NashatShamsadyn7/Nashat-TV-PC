What's inside

Built for how people actually watch

Not a list of checkboxes — every one of these ships in the app today.

Live TV with a real guide

Kurdish and regional channels, sorted by category, with a now-and-next programme guide and a progress bar for whatever is airing.

Movies and series

Browse trending, top rated and now playing, jump by actor, and move to the next episode with one key — season rollover included.

Watch Together

Create a room, share the link, and everyone stays frame-synced. Chat, GIFs, images and a voice channel are built in.

Four channels at once

Multi-view splits the window across several live streams — a match on one tile, the news on another, audio wherever you want it.

Profiles and a real kids mode

Separate profiles behind a PIN. On a kids profile the filtering is enforced on channels and titles alike, with a badge so it never looks broken.

Works with your headphones

Play, pause and skip from AirPods, Bluetooth headsets or the keyboard's media keys — including live streams, where seeking has to follow the buffer.

Picks up where you stopped

Position is saved while you watch and synced to your account, so an episode you paused on one machine resumes on the next.

Friends and messages

Add friends by username, send invites straight into a room, and keep private chats without leaving the app.

Watch Together

Same scene, same second

The host controls playback and everyone else follows. Pause to argue about a plot point, and the room pauses with you.

  • Playback stays in sync for every viewer
  • Chat with GIFs and images over the video
  • Voice channel for the whole room
  • Invite a friend with a single link
